# Employee travel and expense policy

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Status: CURRENT. Employees may claim economy rail or airfare and hotel expenses up to $180 per night before tax. A manager must approve travel before booking. Meals are reimbursed up to $55 per travel day, with itemized receipts.

Claims must be submitted within thirty calendar days after the expense date. Expenses above a stated cap need written manager approval attached to the claim. Personal entertainment and commuting costs are not reimbursable.

This policy applies to employees, not Harbor customer refunds. Customer billing disputes and duplicate charges follow billing-refunds.md, which has its own thirty-day reporting window and review process.
