# Support hours and response targets

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Customer Operations provides standard support Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 America/New_York, excluding company holidays. Standard product questions have a first-response target of one business day on Starter and four business hours on Growth.

Enterprise includes a twenty-four-hour emergency channel for severity-one production incidents, with a first-response target of thirty minutes. Non-emergency Enterprise requests have a two-business-hour target during standard support hours. A response target is not a resolution-time promise.

Severity one means the production service is unavailable for all users of an affected workspace or a critical data exposure is suspected. A single broken chart with an available workaround is severity three. Use the incident response guide to escalate severity-one incidents.
