# Current subscription pricing

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Status: CURRENT. Effective October 1, 2026. All amounts are fictional US dollars per workspace per month before tax.

Starter costs $49 and includes five users, two connected stores and six-hour refreshes. Growth costs $149 and includes twenty users, ten connected stores and hourly refreshes. Enterprise begins at $499, includes fifty users and fifty connected stores, and refreshes every fifteen minutes. Enterprise capacity beyond those allowances requires a written quote. No plan includes unlimited users or stores.

Monthly subscriptions renew monthly. Annual billing receives a ten percent discount on the twelve-month subscription total. Connector add-ons are billed separately and do not receive the annual discount. New customers receive a fourteen-day Growth trial without a payment card. The historical pricing file is superseded by this policy.
