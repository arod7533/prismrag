# Billing and refunds

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Billing is handled by Customer Operations. Customers can cancel renewal at any time; access continues until the paid term ends. Cancellation does not automatically refund the unused portion of a subscription.

A duplicate charge can be refunded in full when reported within thirty calendar days of the charge. Requests should include the workspace ID, invoice number and a description of the duplicate charge. Never include full payment-card details. The billing team reviews complete requests within five business days. Approved refunds are submitted within two business days; bank posting time varies.

Annual subscriptions have the same duplicate-charge rule. Service credits under the SLA are separate from refunds and must be requested through the incident credit process.
