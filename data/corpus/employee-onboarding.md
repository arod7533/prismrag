# Employee access onboarding

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

New Northstar Analytics employees receive an identity-provider account and enroll in MFA before accessing internal systems. Managers request role-based access from the internal access queue; production access is not granted by default.

Customer Operations employees use redacted support views. Platform engineers need a separate approved production role for production work. Access reviews run quarterly. Departing employees have their identity-provider account disabled and active sessions revoked on their final working day.

The training owner is Maya Chen. Every employee completes security awareness training within ten business days of joining. This employee onboarding policy is separate from the customer workspace onboarding process.
