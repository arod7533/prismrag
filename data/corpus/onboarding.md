# Customer onboarding checklist

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

A new Harbor workspace starts with a fourteen-day Growth trial. The workspace owner verifies an email address, selects a region and invites the first administrator. The default region is US East; EU West can be selected at workspace creation.

The recommended onboarding sequence is: invite analysts, connect one store, validate time zones and currency, compare a sample day against the source system, and then publish the first dashboard. Do not connect every store before validating the first import. New data may take up to one scheduled refresh interval to appear.

The onboarding owner is Luis Ortega in Customer Operations. An initial CSV import is limited to fifty megabytes and one million rows per file. Larger imports should use Warehouse Sync.
