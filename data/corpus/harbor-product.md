# Harbor product guide

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Harbor is Northstar Analytics’ retail analytics workspace. It imports sales and inventory from connected stores, lets analysts define metrics, and displays results in shared dashboards. A workspace is the boundary for users, datasets, billing and access controls.

Harbor supports CSV uploads, the CommerceBridge connector and the Warehouse Sync connector. Refreshes run every six hours on Starter, hourly on Growth, and every fifteen minutes on Enterprise. Dashboard exports are CSV or PNG; scheduled PDF delivery is not supported. Harbor does not process card payments or act as a checkout system. Customer Operations owns product questions; Platform Engineering owns connector incidents.
