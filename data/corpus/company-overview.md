# Company overview

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

Northstar Analytics is a fictional company used solely for the PrismRAG portfolio demonstration. It sells Harbor, a hosted analytics workspace for small retail businesses. No policies, customers, certifications or incidents in this corpus refer to a real organization.

Harbor combines store sales, inventory and delivery data into dashboards. The company has three teams: Product, Customer Operations and Platform Engineering. Maya Chen leads Product, Luis Ortega leads Customer Operations, and Priya Shah leads Platform Engineering. The internal knowledge base contains current policies, implementation guides and explicitly marked historical documents. When a historical document conflicts with a current policy, use the current policy.
