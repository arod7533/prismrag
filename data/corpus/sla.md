# Service availability agreement

> Fictional Northstar Analytics demonstration document.

The fictional Enterprise SLA targets 99.9 percent monthly availability for the Harbor dashboard and query service. Starter and Growth do not include contractual availability credits. Scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight hours in advance is excluded, up to four hours per month.

An Enterprise workspace receives a five percent monthly subscription credit when eligible availability is below 99.9 percent but at least 99.0 percent. Availability below 99.0 percent receives a ten percent credit. Total credits are capped at ten percent of that month’s subscription charge and do not apply to connector add-ons.

The Owner must request credits within fifteen calendar days after the affected month ends. Include the workspace ID and incident IDs. Credits apply to a future invoice; they are not cash refunds.
